  2. hint (33 reports) - tenants on booking-v3 (alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour) see MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. Their people type what the hint says, and the form reads it their way. Closed won't fix on 05 Oct: booking-v3 form retired.
